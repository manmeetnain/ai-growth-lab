# Targets

Company names, program details, and event dates below reflect general knowledge and move fast —
verify current status (still running, still accepting applicants, correct dates/URLs) before
sending anything. Treat this as a starting shortlist to research, not a final list to blast.

## Companies likely to run beta / design-partner programs relevant to an agent-evals toolkit

Agent frameworks & runtimes (need eval/reliability partners, often have partner/beta programs):
- LangChain / LangGraph
- CrewAI
- Composio (tool/integration layer for agents)
- E2B (agent sandboxes)
- Modal, Baseten, Fireworks AI, Together AI (inference/hosting infra — care about agent
  reliability at scale)

Evals / observability (closest adjacent space — could be partners or, more realistically,
inspiration/prior art to study and differentiate from, and sometimes run their own
partner/integration programs):
- Braintrust
- Langfuse
- Arize AI
- Galileo
- Weights & Biases (Weave)

MCP ecosystem specifically (directly relevant since MCP is the integration layer the capstone
should target):
- Anthropic's own MCP ecosystem/registry and developer relations channels
- Any MCP-registry or MCP-marketplace startups that emerge — search for these fresh, this
  category is new and changes fast
- Smithery, Glama, and similar MCP directory/registry sites (verify still active)

General AI dev-tool startups with public "apply for early access" pages — check Y Combinator's
current batch list and Product Hunt's AI category for fresh beta programs; these turn over
constantly, so this is a "check monthly" list item, not a one-time lookup.

## Events (verify current dates/CFP status before applying)

- **AI Engineer Summit / World's Fair** (practitioner-focused, agent/tooling-heavy audience —
  strong fit)
- **NeurIPS / ICML / ICLR workshops** (specifically workshop tracks on agents, evaluation, or
  LLM reliability — far more accessible than the main conference track for a first submission)
- **Ray Summit** (if leaning into the infra/scaling angle)
- **KubeCon + CloudNativeCon** (if the tool gets a CI/production-reliability angle)
- Regional/local AI meetups — search Luma and Meetup.com for "AI agents," "LLMOps," "MCP" groups
  in your city/region; these are the easiest first speaking slots and best for direct connections
- **Hackathons** — Devpost, MLH, and company-hosted hackathons (Anthropic, OpenAI, and infra
  startups periodically run these) are a fast, low-commitment way to demo the toolkit to a live
  audience and meet people; use the `hackathon-winner` skill in this session when one comes up to
  turn the capstone into a strong entry rather than starting from scratch each time.

## How to find live beta/design-partner programs (do this monthly, not once)

- Company blogs and changelogs — most beta programs are announced there first
- "early access," "design partner," "pilot program" as search terms alongside the company name
- Y Combinator's public startup directory, filtered to recent batches + AI/dev-tools category
- Product Hunt launches in the AI/dev-tools category — early commenters often get direct founder
  access
- Twitter/X and LinkedIn — founders of small AI startups post beta calls directly; following ~20-
  30 relevant founders is higher signal than any directory

## Webinar co-hosting angle

Target companies from the lists above that have their own webinar series (most infra/dev-tool
startups run monthly or quarterly ones). Pitch: "let me show a live case study of your product +
my toolkit catching a real failure" — this is a low-cost, high-value segment for their webinar
and a direct line to their audience and team for you. See OUTREACH.md for the pitch template.
